PHILOSOPHY OF GOVERNANCE

Statecraft & Political Thought

The political history of ancient Bharatavarsha was guided by sophisticated philosophies of governance balancing Dharma (righteous order), Artha (economic prosperity), and Rajadharma (duty of the sovereign).

Kautilya's Arthashastra

Authored by Chanakya (Kautilya), the Arthashastra provided a masterwork on statecraft, espionage, economic administration, municipal taxation, and the Saptanga (seven limbs) model of state sovereignty.

Sabha & Santhagara Democracy

Republican Gana-Sanghas like the Vajji and Shakya clans governed through elected assemblies. Decisions were reached via voting sticks (Salaka) and public consensus in council halls (Santhagaras).

Mandala Theory of Diplomacy

Ancient Indian diplomacy utilized the Mandala system—a strategic framework of geopolitical concentric circles detailing alliances, balance of power, and neutral state relations.
